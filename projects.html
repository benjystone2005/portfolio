Independent equity research · June 2026

IAG / Avios: Loyalty Breakage Research Memo

Airlines book profit when loyalty points expire unredeemed, an estimate called “breakage.” This memo asks a simple question the sell side hasn't: how much of IAG's reported profit rests on that assumption, and is anyone checking it?

€2,630m → €3,348m

Avios liability growth over four years, up 27% from 2022 to 2025

€51m → €285m

Unexplained residual in the liability movement: roughly fourfold growth

€103m

Profit impact of a 5-point shift in the breakage assumption, per IAG's own disclosure

~14 points

The assumption shift the 2025 residual is equivalent to (far beyond normal year-on-year variation)

The research didn't produce a confirmed short. Open questions remain, including whether the residual has a legitimate accounting explanation. But that was the point: the value was in the process. Finding a subjective, growing profit lever that analysts treat as an unqualified positive, quantifying its sensitivity from primary disclosures, and knowing when the evidence doesn't yet clear the bar for a trade.

The Luxury Watch Index is an equally-weighted index tracking secondary market prices across 10 luxury watch references, sourced consistently from Chrono24. Two to three times a month, Benjy calculates the median Ask price for each constituent and normalises it against the base date to produce a composite index: the same discipline, update after update, since launch.

The index lives on an interactive site with live charts, YTD performance and full methodology documentation.
