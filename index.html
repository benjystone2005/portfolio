IAG / Avios: Loyalty Breakage Research Memo

How much IAG’s profit moves if its breakage estimate changes by 5 points, by IAG’s own numbers

When Avios points expire unused, IAG books their value as profit, based on its own estimate of how many will never be spent. I wanted to know how much profit rests on that estimate, and whether anyone outside the company checks it.
